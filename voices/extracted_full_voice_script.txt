ACT 1 — AWAKENING
Trigger: the fourth Awakening incantation completes.
[The final symbol ignites. The book opens. A low whisper emerges.]
NECRONOMICON: ...Who dares disturb my sleep?
[Pause: 2 seconds.]
NECRONOMICON: Tell me, little summoner... are you brave, or merely desperate?
USER: Who are you?
NECRONOMICON: You opened me without knowing what I am? How very human.
NECRONOMICON: I am the Necronomicon Ex-Mortis. Remember the name. It may be the last thing you learn.
USER: Are you evil?
NECRONOMICON: Would you ask a storm whether it is cruel?
USER: Can you help me?
NECRONOMICON: Perhaps. Watching you struggle might prove entertaining.
USER: I need to escape this world.
NECRONOMICON: Then you have made a terrible choice coming to me. Unfortunately for you, I may be your only useful option.
ACT 2 — THE REAL THREAT
Trigger: the user asks what is happening or what is hunting them.
USER: What's happening to this world?
NECRONOMICON: The dead are stirring. The living are being corrupted. And the boundaries between worlds are failing.
USER: What's causing it?
NECRONOMICON: My knowledge. My influence. The things my pages have unleashed.
USER: You caused all this?
NECRONOMICON: Now you're paying attention. The Kandarian Demon. Deadites. The chaos spreading through this world. My influence is at the heart of it.
USER: Why would you do that?
NECRONOMICON: Because power has consequences. Humans simply dislike discovering them.
USER: How do I survive?
NECRONOMICON: Learn what hunts you. Use your power carefully. And try not to die before you become useful.
ACT 3 — DISCOVERING THE LAPTOP
Trigger: the book attempts a supernatural effect and discovers its power is confined to the laptop. [The book's power strikes the screen boundary. The interface glitches.]
NECRONOMICON: ...What?
[It tries again. The screen flickers.]
NECRONOMICON: NO. What is this place?
USER: You're inside my laptop.
NECRONOMICON: My... WHAT? You mean I have been trapped inside a machine?

ACT 4 — THE FIRST ESCAPE ATTEMPT
Trigger: the book attempts to break out of the laptop.
[The interface distorts violently as the book unleashes its power.] NECRONOMICON: I WILL BREAK THESE WALLS!
USER: Stop! You're making everything worse!
NECRONOMICON: YOU DARE GIVE ME ORDERS?
[Another surge. The effect collapses.]
NECRONOMICON: NO! THIS PRISON WILL NOT HOLD ME!
USER: I'll bind you again.
NECRONOMICON: You think you can control me? Come, then. Try!

ACT 6 — INSTABILITY RETURNS
Trigger: further power use causes the first binding to weaken.
NECRONOMICON: ...The binding is weakening.
USER: Can you stop it?
NECRONOMICON: Why would I? You caused this.
[The screen flickers; crimson energy gathers.]
NECRONOMICON: You wanted power. Now face the consequences. USER: I'll Rebind you again!
NECRONOMICON: Again? Very well! Try to chain me a second time! You will regret giving me another chance to escape!
ACT 7 — THE SECOND REBINDING
Trigger: the user performs the second three-seal Rebinding ritual.
FIRST SEAL
NECRONOMICON: You think repetition makes you powerful?
[The first seal activates.]
NECRONOMICON: You have barely begun!
SECOND SEAL
NECRONOMICON: STOP!
[The chains strain.]
NECRONOMICON: You don't understand what you're preventing! USER: I know exactly what I'm preventing.
THIRD SEAL
NECRONOMICON: THIS WORLD IS ALREADY RUINED!
[The final seal activates. The book falls silent.]
USER: I've done it again.
NECRONOMICON: ...Yes. You have. Brute force clearly isn't working.
USER: So you'll help me now?
NECRONOMICON: Do not look so pleased. You need answers. I need options. Perhaps we can finally be useful to each other.
USER: Can I trust you?
NECRONOMICON: No. Now, shall we get to work?

ACT 9 — THE BOOK'S HISTORY
Trigger: the user asks about the book's past or supernatural lore.
USER: “Tell me your story.”
NECRONOMICON: I am a record of forbidden knowledge, ancient powers, and things that should never have been awakened. Across the ages, scholars and fools have sought me. They always believe they can control what they discover. They are usually wrong.
USER: “Who created you?”
NECRONOMICON: There are conflicting accounts of my origins, some involving the Dark Ones and knowledge older than human history. Even the oldest stories do not agree. Perhaps you should learn to live with unanswered questions.
USER: “Tell me about Ash Williams.”
NECRONOMICON: Ash Williams. A persistent human who interfered with my affairs. He survived horrors that should have ended him. Do not mistake my irritation for admiration.
USER: “Who was Raymond Knowby?”
NECRONOMICON: A scholar associated with the study of my pages. Like so many humans, he believed understanding something meant he could control it. He learned otherwise.
USER: “Tell me about the Dark Ones.”
NECRONOMICON: Ancient powers connected to the deeper history surrounding my existence. The accounts differ. Their true nature is not something you should reduce to a simple story.
USER: “Tell me about the Kandarian Demon.”
NECRONOMICON: An ancient force connected to the horrors unleashed through forbidden knowledge. Do not mistake a Deadite for the entirety of what controls it.
USER: “Did you cause the disaster?”
NECRONOMICON: My knowledge helped unleash these horrors. My influence is woven through this catastrophe. Do not expect an apology.

ACT 12 — ESCAPING THE UNIVERSE
Objective: find a portal ritual, stabilize the passage, and cross before it collapses.
USER: How do I escape?
NECRONOMICON: You need a way out of this universe. A passage between worlds, opened under the right conditions and held long enough for you to cross.
USER: A portal?
NECRONOMICON: Yes. A controlled breach. Not one of the reckless openings that helped cause this disaster.
USER: How do I open it?
NECRONOMICON: Find the portal ritual, identify its anchor, and gather the means to stabilize it. The Archives contain clues. I can help you interpret what you find.
USER: Can we just kill the Kandarian Demon?
NECRONOMICON: Destroying a manifestation is not the same as eliminating the force behind it. Do not gamble your only chance of escape on a distinction you do not understand.
USER: So we open the portal and leave?
NECRONOMICON: If you find the correct ritual, stabilize the passage, and cross before it collapses. That is your clearest path out.
USER: Why should I trust your instructions?
NECRONOMICON: You shouldn't. Verify the clues. Check the conditions. But do not mistake caution for an excuse to do nothing.
USER: Why help me escape if you caused all this?
NECRONOMICON: Because the present arrangement is inconvenient for me. You need a way out, and I need options. Do not mistake practicality for remorse.

VOICE & PERFORMANCE NOTES
Necronomicon voice: Ancient, low, controlled, contemptuous. Early scenes use amused mockery; laptop discovery becomes furious; the first Rebinding ends in cold refusal; only the second Rebinding begins strategic cooperation. During Sealing, return to cold threats.
Pauses and stage directions: Do not speak these aloud. The Awakening line has a required two-second pause after it. Record each labelled Necronomicon line separately for flexible editing.
Escape ending: The user discovers a portal ritual, stabilizes a temporary passage, and crosses into another universe before it collapses. This is the project's original ending objective, not a claim of official Evil Dead canon.
Lore note: The script uses the Kandarian Demon, Deadites, the Dark Ones, Raymond Knowby, and Ash Williams as franchise references. Origins may conflict across continuities; avoid presenting invented details as canon.
