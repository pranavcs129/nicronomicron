Necronomicon Ex-Mortis: Final Script Draft v2
Full conversation and voice script for the digital Necronomicon experience. Every spoken line has an ID, an emotion tag and, where needed, variants. Stage directions in [brackets] are never recorded.
0. What changed in v3
Final direction (v3): one Rebinding, one central threat, one meaningful solution, one dangerous book.
This version simplifies the project around its core without removing what makes the book feel alive.
One Rebinding. The second Rebinding, the repeatable containment loop and the optional preventative Rebinding are cut. Instability is now one meter with a warning state and a critical state.
The turn replaces the second Rebinding. After the single Rebinding the book refuses help. When the threat becomes impossible to ignore, it shifts to calculated cooperation (section 8).
No final choice. The timed branching sequence is cut. The book's last betrayal is a short beat with no choice before the Sealing (section 14).
Vision and anomaly are not scripted here. Section 11 is reserved. The script exposes one hook, threat_event , so the separate concept can plug in later without changing this script.
Simple ability rules. Three abilities with simple prerequisites. Casting-Out stays locked until the manifestation is weakened and the breach is stable.
Kept: the four-incantation Awakening, the laptop discovery, session memory, a small connected set of clues, the portal counter-ritual, the four-incantation Sealing, and a small Q&A library with a reliable fallback.
Principle. Flexible dialogue, deterministic progression. The player controls the conversation. The system controls the essential story conditions (section 18.4).

Principle: flexible dialogue, deterministic progression. The player steers the conversation and the book answers in many ways. The system alone decides the essential story conditions: which state the story is in, what unlocks, and when each beat fires. Details are in section 18.4.

16. Voice and performance notes
Voice. Ancient, low, controlled, contemptuous. Early scenes use amused mockery. The laptop discovery becomes furious. The first Rebinding ends in cold refusal. Only the arrival of the threat begins strategic cooperation. The portal ritual is tense, self-interested and slightly too casual. Sealing returns to cold threats.
Emotion contrast. The quiet lines ("...Lucky attempt.") must be genuinely quiet so the shouting lands. Record furious lines last in each session.
Pauses and stage directions. Never spoken. The Awakening pause is two seconds, built into the engine.
Processing (after recording). Lower pitch by roughly 2 to 4 semitones, a subtle doubled layer an octave lower mixed low, short dark reverb, light saturation on furious lines only. Normalize all lines to the same loudness.
Technical. Record each line separately in a quiet room with a pop filter. Export WAV, then compress to MP3 or OGG. Sound effects (seals, chains, flicker) are separate files. Always show subtitles on screen as a fallback.

18.4 Flexible dialogue, deterministic progression
The player controls the conversation. The system controls the essential story conditions. The result is a living book with a reliable demo.

     Rules
1. Words never move the story. Story state changes only through system events: a completed ritual, a flag, a timer or a button tap. No phrase the player types can unlock an ability, skip a gate or end the ritual.
2. Same state, same outcome. Variants change the wording only. Every run passes through the same states in the same order, so the demo behaves the same each time.
3. Clues unlock in order. Clue 1 unlocks at the turn, clue 2 after clue 1, clue 3 after clue 2. Dead Sight needs clue 1 and Binding Word needs clue 2.
4. Every clue has two routes. The player can ask (by keyword or by tapping a chip), or the book volunteers it. If a clue has been unlocked for 60 seconds and not found, the book plays CL_OFFER and then the clue.
